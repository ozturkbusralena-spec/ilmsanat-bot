Ilm Sanat Publisher: Terms and Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

Terms of Service Privacy Policy

Ilm Sanat Publisher is a private tool used by the Ilm Sanat account to prepare its own posts on geopolitics, history and Orientalist art, and to send them to its own Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Terms of Service

Who may use it

Only the owner of the Ilm Sanat accounts uses this tool. Nobody else can sign in or connect an account.

What it does

The tool uploads images and videos created for Ilm Sanat to the Ilm Sanat Instagram account and to the Ilm Sanat TikTok account as drafts. The account owner reviews each draft and decides whether to publish it.

Platform rules

All content follows the Instagram Terms of Use, the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Community Guidelines. News summaries are written in our own words and name their source.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, for the internal use of Ilm Sanat. These terms may change; the date above shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

Data we access

  • From TikTok: the basic profile of the Ilm Sanat account (open ID, display name, avatar), needed to upload drafts to it.
  • From Instagram: the profile, posts and insights of the Ilm Sanat account, needed to publish posts and see how they perform.

Data we do not collect

The tool does not collect, store or process data about followers, viewers or any other TikTok or Instagram users.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer or server, in a private file that is never shared or published. Nothing is sold or passed to third parties.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time in the Instagram and TikTok settings under connected apps. When access is withdrawn, the stored tokens are deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent as a direct message to @ilmsanat on Instagram.
