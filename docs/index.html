Ilm Sanat

Ilm Sanat Publisher

Ilm Sanat shares short, sourced videos on Middle East and African geopolitics, political history and Orientalist art on Instagram and TikTok.

Ilm Sanat Publisher (Google Cloud project “IlmSanat Otomasyon”) is the private tool that prepares these videos and sends them to the Ilm Sanat accounts. It uses Google Drive only to hold its own videos for a short time so Instagram can fetch them. It has no other users.
